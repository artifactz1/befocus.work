## Phase Goal

**As a** person running a session, **I want to** glance at the top-left of the screen and see the
whole session (done, now, ahead, added time, ticked tasks) in the view I prefer **so that** I know
where I am without opening anything.

<objective>
Replace the top-left grid slot with a switchable progress slot (Strip / Now / Grid) driven by a new
`sessionProgress` Look preference, a switcher popover that also opens the Session card, the
matching Customize control, and the idle dim rule.

Purpose: TSK-04 (live progress at a glance, desktop; phone sizes land in 09-11).
Seams consumed (09-CONTEXT "Dependencies and Seams"): **Block list in the store** (through
useSessionView, mapped in 09-07) and **Preference persistence** (Phase 3 settings persistence for
signed-in users, browser storage for guests). Task 1 checks the Phase 3 side.
Output: Look key and Customize control, strip.ts plus check, ProgressSlot and its CSS module, Header
and globals.css idle changes.
</objective>
