## Phase Goal

**As a** person running focus sessions, **I want to** add or remove a minute from the footer and
see how far past my plan I am, **so that** I can stretch or trim a block without losing track.

<objective>
Fourth slice: the pointer path for adjusting and its feedback. The footer's outer slots become
−1 / +1 while running and ended (dimmed per the states table), the digits get a net badge, the
three progress styles rescale and mark the planned end, and every adjustment from any input is
announced in the single live region added in 08-03.

Purpose: BLK-02 (add time while running and at 00:00, visible and accessible).
Output: TimerButtons.tsx, Timer.tsx and timer-progress.module.css edits.
</objective>
