  <action>
useHoldPress({ onTap, onHoldOpen, onRelease }) returns pointer handlers for a trigger button and a
`held` boolean, per the UI-SPEC Gesture table: pointerdown (primary button only) calls
`setPointerCapture`, sets held and starts a 400 ms timer; pointerup before the timer is a tap
(onTap, which applies 1 minute; presses stack); pointerleave of the button rect (check with the
event coordinates, since capture keeps events on the trigger) or pointercancel before 400 ms
cancels; at 400 ms onHoldOpen fires and nothing is applied. After opening, pointermove uses
`document.elementFromPoint` to find a `[role='menuitem']` under the pointer and reports it as the
active cell; pointerup over an enabled cell calls onRelease(minutes); over a dimmed cell or off any
cell nothing applies and the tray stays open. Native click after a pointer gesture is suppressed so
a tap never double-applies; keyboard Enter / Space keep native click (apply 1 minute).

AdjustTray({ id, sign: 1 | -1, open, activeMinutes, openedBy: 'pointer' | 'keyboard', onApply,
onClose, triggerRef }): renders, when open, `<div id={id} role="menu" aria-label="Add time" |
"Remove time">` with three `role="menuitem"` buttons for 1 / 5 / 10 (visible "+1 +5 +10" or
"−1 −5 −10" with U+2212; aria-labels from the Copywriting Contract). Remove cells are
aria-disabled when `m * 60 >= timeLeft` (read from the store, re-evaluated each tick); dimmed cells
are focusable but inert. Keyboard inside the menu (the component owns these): ArrowLeft /
ArrowRight move and wrap; Enter / Space apply the focused enabled cell, close and focus the
trigger; Escape / Tab close and focus the trigger (Tab does not preventDefault). Opened by keyboard
it focuses the first enabled cell; opened by pointer focus stays on the trigger. A window
`keydown` Escape listener and a `pointerdown` outside both the tray and the trigger close it while
open (covers the pointer-opened case). Also export `readAdjustHintSeen()` (readStored
'befocus.adjustHintSeen' with z.boolean(), false when null) and `markAdjustHintSeen()` (writeStored
true, then `window.dispatchEvent(new Event('befocus:adjust-hint-seen'))`), per the UI-SPEC key name.

adjust-tray.module.css: the UI-SPEC Tray layout values (absolute, bottom calc(100% + 8px), −1 tray
left 0, +1 tray right 0, flex gap 4px padding 4px radius 12px, the panel surface and shadow,
72x44 cells radius 8px, 52x44 and 14px under max-width 1023.98px or pointer coarse, active cell
foreground fill with #101010 text, dimmed 0.3, z-index 40, focus outline), open motion (120ms
opacity plus translateY(4px) to 0, close 80ms, instant under reduced motion), `.held` (rgb(255 255
255 / 0.12) background, 0.28 border) and `.holdable` (touch-action none, user-select none,
-webkit-user-select none, -webkit-touch-callout none).
