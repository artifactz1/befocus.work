## Phase Goal

**As a** person focusing with BeFocus, **I want to** pick a record off the shelf and watch it spin on the
turntable, **so that** choosing music feels tactile and calm.

<objective>
Fill the room's main area with the shelf and the turntable: generated label inks, click and drag to
load, one record at a time enforced in the store, spin and tonearm motion, play/pause, record volume,
playback error handling, and the shelf's loading, error and empty states.

Purpose: VNL-03 (shelf) and VNL-04 (load, spin, one at a time).
Output: labelInk + playRecordState helpers with checks, store record actions, Shelf.tsx, Turntable.tsx.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-10 | Spoofing | Turntable onDrop | mitigate | Only the custom MIME type is accepted, and the id must exist in the store as a bgMusic sound; anything else is ignored |
