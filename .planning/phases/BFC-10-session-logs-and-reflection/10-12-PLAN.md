## Phase Goal

**As a** person who works in focus blocks, **I want to** note what got done at each break and see an
honest summary and history of my sessions, **so that** I can see what I actually did and what to
change next time.

<objective>
The What to improve panel that tops /log.

Purpose: LOG-05 (simple observations from the user's own history).
Seams consumed: `HistorySession` (10-05 history.ts), `improveState`, `observations`, `planVsActual`,
`calendar`, `Observation` (10-05 insights.ts), `makeFormat` (10-04), `Seg` (10-09), `readStored`
/ `writeStored` (lib/storage/browser-storage.ts).
Output: components/logs/ImprovePanel.tsx and improve.module.css.

Discretion decisions recorded here:
- Default view "Insights" (UI-SPEC discretion). The stored pick is read in an effect after mount so
  SSR and the first client render agree.
- Phone limits (2 observations, 4 chart rows) are applied with CSS `:nth-child` rules under
  1024px, not a JS media query, so there is no layout flash.
- The panel receives `sessions` and `now` from LogPage (10-13) and does no fetching.
</objective>
