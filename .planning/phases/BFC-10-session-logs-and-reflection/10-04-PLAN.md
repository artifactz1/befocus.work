## Phase Goal

**As a** person who works in focus blocks, **I want to** note what got done at each break and see an
honest summary and history of my sessions, **so that** I can see what I actually did and what to
change next time.

<objective>
Build the pure logic of the end-of-session summary and its export, test first, with no React and no
storage: the shape every summary surface renders and the text every export writes.

Purpose: LOG-03 (summary from that session's records), LOG-07 (done / not done / later with fates),
LOG-08 (copy / download content).
Seams consumed: Block record (`toRecord`, `mergeRecords`), Plan and links (`TaskLink`), Tasks
(`Task` with `later` / `laterAt` from 10-01).
Output: lib/logs/summary.ts, lib/logs/export.ts and their two check scripts.

Discretion decisions recorded here:
- A session is "finished" when it has a session note row (written when the summary first appears,
  10-10); otherwise "stopped". Block totals come from the records.
- A break record with outcome 'skipped' counts toward "breaks cut short"; its row reads "cut short"
  when it ran 60 s or more and "skipped" otherwise.
- The Later list is computed only for the live summary (`live: true`); past sessions show
  read-only fate tags from the note (UI-SPEC section 3, "Past sessions").
- Formatting goes through `makeFormat(locale?, timeZone?)` so the checks can pin 'en-GB' and 'UTC';
  the app passes nothing and gets the browser locale.
</objective>
