## Phase Goal

**As a** person who works in focus blocks, **I want to** note what got done at each break and see an
honest summary and history of my sessions, **so that** I can see what I actually did and what to
change next time.

<objective>
The summary view itself, shared by the stage (10-11) and the Log page (10-13).

Purpose: LOG-03 (summary of that session), LOG-07 (done / not done / later list), LOG-08 (export),
LOG-02 (edit a saved log).
Seams consumed: `buildSummary`, `makeFormat`, `Summary`, `SummaryBlock` (10-04), `toMarkdown`,
`toText`, `exportFileName` (10-04), `useLogActions` (10-06), `LogForm`, `Seg` (10-09), Phase 9
`AddRow` (ListTab.tsx), `@repo/ui/dropdown-menu`, sonner.
Output: hooks/useSummary.ts and five files under components/logs.

Discretion decisions recorded here:
- SessionSummary is presentational: it takes `summary`, `live`, `handlers`, `actions` (a ReactNode
  for the actions row, supplied by the stage or /log) and `autoOpenLast`. Fate writes (which need
  plan actions) are supplied by the stage through `handlers.setFate`; on /log `setFate` is absent
  and the list shows read-only tags.
- `useSummaryModel({ mode, store, input })` holds optimistic overrides for logs, note
  (nextTime, extraDone, carried) and ticks, rebuilds the summary with buildSummary, and saves
  through useLogActions. Next time saves on blur (no toast).
- Share is shown only after mount when `typeof navigator.share === 'function'`; a rejected share
  (other than AbortError) falls back to copying Markdown.
</objective>
