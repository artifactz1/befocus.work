## Phase Goal

**As a** person who works in focus blocks, **I want to** note what got done at each break and see an
honest summary and history of my sessions, **so that** I can see what I actually did and what to
change next time.

<objective>
The break log: shared form, segmented control and the modal over the timer.

Purpose: LOG-01 (prompt at the break, skippable, break keeps running), LOG-02 (save a log with
text, ticks, rating and carry-over).
Seams consumed: `breakPromptFor` (10-08), `active.askAtBreak` (10-02), `useLogActions` (10-06),
`useTaskActions().setLater` (10-07), `usePlanActions().assignTask` (Phase 9), the restart-confirm
shell in `session-card.module.css`, the keyboard guard attribute `data-break-log` (10-02).
Output: components/logs/{Seg,LogForm,BreakLogModal}.tsx, logs.module.css, one mount line in
TasksCard.tsx.

Discretion decisions recorded here:
- Built on `@radix-ui/react-dialog` (already used by SessionCard) for focus trap and focus restore;
  `onPointerDownOutside` and `onInteractOutside` call `preventDefault()`; `onEscapeKeyDown` and
  the X act as Skip.
- The modal mounts in TasksCard (always mounted inside both shells, next to `usePlanSync`) so it
  has the tasks, timer and customize stores.
- Only changed ticks are written; a tick is "changed" when it differs from the link's `completed`
  at open.
- The Move to default is the next remaining focus block; a task whose selection equals its current
  planned block is not re-written.
</objective>
