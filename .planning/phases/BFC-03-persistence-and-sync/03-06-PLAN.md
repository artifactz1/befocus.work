## Phase Goal

**As a** person who sets up BeFocus to look the way I like, **I want** my look to still be there
the next time I open the app, on this device or another one I sign in on, **so that** I never redo
it and never see the default flash before my look appears.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 3 goal.)

evidence (screenshot path in the session scratchpad, network request, or console line).

1. **Guest restore (SYN-03, ENG-05)**
   - Open /guest in a fresh context and change background, accent and progress style. Press Done,
     then reload.
   - The look is the same, and Revert is disabled when the panel opens.
   - Run a throttled hard reload (CPU 4x, Slow 3G) with a performance trace. The first frame with
     content has the saved background and accent. Record the frame timestamps.
2. **Guest note (UI-SPEC section 2)**
   - At 1440, the note sits above Revert / Done and wraps to two lines.
   - At 390, the note is the sheet's last row, with no clipping or horizontal scroll.
   - Tab reaches the link and the ring shows. Click it: you land on /sign-in. Back on /guest the
     last change is kept.
3. **Storage blocked (UI-4 A)**
   - Block site data for localhost, or override `localStorage` to throw with an init script.
   - Press Done with a change: one neutral toast. A second Done with another change shows no
     second toast. Reloading shows the default look.
4. **Signed-in restore (SYN-01, ENG-05)**
   - Sign up, change the look, press Done. One `PUT /user/themes/active` returns 200 with no toast.
   - Reload `/`: same look. Repeat the throttled trace from check 1.
   - Close the panel with Escape and with the X button after another change. Each sends one PUT.
5. **Second device (SYN-02)**
   - In a second isolated browser context (new profile or incognito-equivalent), sign in as the
     same user.
   - The first frame of `/` wears the look, and the guest cache in that context is empty.
6. **Rapid Apply (SYN-05)**
   - With Slow 3G, open, change background and press Done five times quickly with five different
     backgrounds.
   - The network shows at most two PUTs in flight in sequence: the first, then the latest.
   - After the requests settle, reload: the fifth background shows.
7. **Save failed (UI-1 A)**
   - Set network to offline, change the look and press Done. You get one error toast with the
     exact copy, and the look stays painted.
   - Back online, change and press Done again. The PUT returns 200 with no new toast.
8. **Sign-out leak check**
   - Sign out (soft navigation via `router.push('/')`, RESEARCH A2) and land on /guest.
   - The guest page shows the guest look (or the default), not the account look, without a hard
     reload. If the account look lingers, record it as a bug and fix it inside this phase's files.
9. **Console (RESEARCH A3)**
   - Record any React warning about the inline script, and any hydration mismatch, on /, /guest
     and /sign-in. A hydration mismatch is a bug to fix. A known benign script warning is recorded
     with its exact text.
10. **Pixel pass**
    - At 1440 and 390, compare the panel, the note and both toasts against UI-SPEC.
    - Fix anything that looks off, even if unrelated, and list it in the SUMMARY.

Close every browser context afterwards. Leave the throwaway accounts in place and list their
emails in the SUMMARY so a human can delete them.
