## Phase Goal

**As a** person who sets up BeFocus to look the way I like, **I want** my look to still be there
the next time I open the app, on this device or another one I sign in on, **so that** I never redo
it and never see the default flash before my look appears.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 3 goal.)
